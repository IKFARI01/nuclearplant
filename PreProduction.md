# My Inspiration

I chose to take inspiration from the nuclear disaster in Chrenobyl, though not nessesarily in it's destroyed state, as not only was it extremely difficult to procure images of the most heavily impacted areas, especially the desrtoyed quarter itself, and the elephants foot would have been extremely difficult to recreate, so I decided to approach the map with elements of what Chrenobyl looked like before the disasters. The map you may notice, is in shape very similar to the layout of the Chrenobyl plant's own blueprint, if not simplified.
